About Me

Hi, I'm Kat, the face behind The Painting Pixie. I absolutely love bringing colour, creativity and imagination to children's parties, weddings, festivals and community events.

Known for my warm, patient and positive approach, I quickly build a rapport with children, helping even the shyest little ones feel comfortable and excited to join in. Every design is created using premium-quality, skin-safe paints and plenty of sparkle.

Whether it's a superhero transformation, a magical unicorn or an intricate butterfly, my goal is always the same – to create unforgettable memories and lots of happy smiles.
