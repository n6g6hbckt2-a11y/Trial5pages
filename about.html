About The Painting Pixie

Hi, I’m Kat 👋

I’m the face behind The Painting Pixie, bringing colour, creativity and imagination to children’s parties, weddings, festivals and community events.

I’m known for my warm, patient and friendly approach, helping even the shyest children feel relaxed and excited to join in. Every design is created using high-quality, skin-safe paints with plenty of sparkle.

From superhero transformations and magical unicorns to butterflies, tigers and custom designs, my goal is always the same — to create unforgettable memories and lots of happy smiles.
